# Draw the light

Refract is a puzzle of routes, light, and careful choices. Each optical bench is a lattice of emitters, lenses, and glowing crystals, with every shape carrying a clue to what it can do.

Press an emitter and trace a beam across adjacent nodes. Reach its partner emitter and thread every lens of the same shape. The beam follows your hand one node at a time, while moves that break the board's rules simply hold in place.

Crystals are shared by every color of light. Each crossing spends one charge, and a board is complete only when every crystal is spent and every beam has finished its route.

Campaign offers 24 hand-built boards that grow in size and complexity. Cascade deals a continuing stream of generated puzzles and raises the challenge as you solve them.
